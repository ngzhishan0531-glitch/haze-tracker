How alerts work

Alerts fire when the reading rises from below your threshold to at or above it, and again only after it has dropped below and crossed back.

This app has no server, so checks run on your phone: while the app is open, and in the background on Android Chrome when the app is installed (timing is up to the browser, often hourly). iPhone: add to Home Screen first (iOS 16.4+), then enable notifications.

Data: NEA via data.gov.sg, Singapore Open Data Licence. PSI updates hourly. 1-hr reading is PM2.5 concentration, as NEA publishes.
